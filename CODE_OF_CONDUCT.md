# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's data, credentials, or private communications.
- Ask for clarification before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or community spaces.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, and events where you represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's listed contact or a private GitHub security advisory if the report involves sensitive information. Do not open a public issue about a personal incident.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from the project. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by other maintainers.

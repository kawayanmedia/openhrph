# Government records

This page covers the reference and official-record screens in the **Government** menu: Salary Grades,
NOSA, Service Records, and Leave Cards. The PDS/201 file is covered in
[Reports and payslips](../shared/reports-and-payslips.md).

## Salary grades

**Government → Salary Grades**

Salary grades are the reference table used by plantilla items and appointments. Keep the grade and step
values aligned with the authorized salary schedule. Do not edit a historical grade to change past
appointments; update the schedule for future effectivity.

## NOSA

**Government → NOSA**

Record Notice of Salary Adjustment (NOSA) entries:

- employee and position,
- salary grade and step,
- effectivity date,
- reference and approving authority,
- supporting document.

NOSA records support payroll changes and the employee's service record. Keep the effectivity date accurate;
payroll uses it to decide when the new salary applies.

## Service records

**Government → Service Records**

The service record is the employee's official service history. It includes appointments, status changes,
and other recorded movements.

1. Review the service record after each validated appointment or completed personnel action.
2. Correct errors through the appropriate government process, using effective dates and supporting
   documents.
3. Do not delete history. If a record is wrong, add a correcting entry or update the source appointment and
   let the record regenerate.

Service records are used by retirement eligibility, GSIS claims, and terminal-leave review.

## Leave cards

**Government → Leave Cards**

Leave cards record government leave balances and entries. They support:

- leave application review,
- terminal-leave computation at retirement,
- audits of leave credits and usage.

Check the leave card before approving terminal leave. The retirement calculation records the reviewed
days and daily rate; it does not replace the official leave card.

## PDS and 201 file

The employee's PDS and 201 file are generated from the employee record and the government records above.
Open them from the employee record or **Reports → Employee Master File**. See
[Reports and payslips](../shared/reports-and-payslips.md#201-file).

## Privacy and retention

Service records, leave cards, NOSA entries, and PDS data are sensitive. Limit access to HR staff who need
them. Retain records according to your agency's records-retention schedule; do not delete official records
to correct a mistake.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Salary grade is missing from a plantilla item | The salary grade reference must exist and be effective. |
| NOSA is not reflected in payroll | Check the effectivity date and whether payroll has been reopened/reprocessed. |
| Service record is missing an appointment | The appointment must be validated and the personnel action completed. |
| Leave card balance differs from the portal | Confirm the leave card entries and the leave ledger; the leave card is the official government record. |
| 201 file is missing government data | Check the employee record, appointment, service record, and leave card. |

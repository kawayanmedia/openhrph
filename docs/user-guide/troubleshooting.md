# Troubleshooting

This page helps you solve common problems. If a problem could affect payroll or employee data, contact your administrator before making changes.

## I cannot sign in

- Check the email address and password.
- Check that Caps Lock is off.
- Use **Show** on the password field to confirm what you typed.
- Wait a minute if you have tried several times; sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
- Ask your administrator to confirm your account is active and has admin access.

If the page says your account does not have access, your group is missing an admin permission. Ask an administrator to update your group or make you a company owner.

## I can sign in but a menu item is missing

Menu entries are filtered by your group's permissions. Ask an administrator to check the permission for that area. A company owner can usually see everything.

## I see no records

- Check the company switcher at the top of the screen. You may be in the wrong company.
- Clear the search box and filters.
- Check whether the records belong to another company or were deleted.

## A form will not save

- Look for the red error message under the field.
- Fields marked with **\*** are required.
- Check number fields for letters or unsupported symbols.
- Check date fields for a valid date.
- If the error mentions a duplicate value, use a different code, employee number, or email.

## A payroll will not process

1. Check the payroll status. An approved payroll must be reopened by a company owner before it can be processed again.
2. Check that employees are assigned to the payroll group and have attendance for the period.
3. Check the error message on the payroll page.
4. Ask your administrator to confirm the payroll engine is available and the queue worker is running.
5. Try **Preview** again after fixing the data.

Do not approve a payroll that shows unexpected totals.

## A payslip is missing

- Process the payroll first. Payslips are available after processing.
- Check the employee is in the payroll register.
- Check the employee belongs to the current company.
- If the page does not open, ask your administrator to check file storage and permissions.

## An import fails

- Start with a small test file.
- Keep the column names exactly as the template requires.
- Remove blank rows and duplicate employee numbers.
- Save the file as the format requested by the import.
- Read the error message; it usually names the row or column that needs fixing.

## The page looks wrong after an update

- Refresh the page.
- Clear the browser cache or try a private window.
- Try another modern browser.
- If the layout is broken for everyone, ask your administrator to rebuild the front-end assets.

## The theme does not change

Select the sun or moon icon in the header. The choice is stored in your browser. If it does not persist, check that your browser allows local storage.

## What to tell your administrator

Include:

- Your name, email, and company.
- The page or menu you were using.
- What you expected to happen.
- What actually happened.
- The exact error message, if any.
- The date and time of the problem.
- A screenshot with personal data masked if possible.

Never send passwords or full employee records in a support message.

## When to stop and ask for help

Stop and contact your administrator if:

- payroll totals look wrong
- employee data is missing or duplicated
- you suspect another company's data is visible
- you cannot sign in as an owner
- a payroll is locked and you need to correct it
- you receive a security warning from your browser

# Private-sector HR

:::info You are in the Private-sector guide

The sidebar on this page lists the Private modules plus the modules both sectors share. If your installation
is Government, switch to the [Government-sector user guide](../government/government-hr.md).

:::

This is the entry point for Private-sector organisations. Shared modules — organization, documents,
approvals, corrections, performance, learning, retirement mechanics, compliance, and analytics — are
listed in [HR, workforce and talent overview](../shared/hr-and-workforce.md).

The private menus appear only when the installation's sector is **Private**. If they are missing, ask your
administrator to check **Settings → Sector Settings**.

## How the private workflow fits together

```text
Recruitment → Hire / Convert → Onboarding → Employment → Payroll
                                                    ↓
                                              Separation → Clearance → Final settlement
```

- **Recruitment** ends with a hired application. The hire conversion creates the employee, employment, and
  onboarding checklist.
- **Employment** owns the ongoing relationship: contracts, probation, regularization, and private leave
  rules.
- **Separation** owns clearance and the reviewed final settlement, and marks the employee separated when
  the case becomes effective.
- **Retirement** is used when the separation is a retirement; the benefit and terminal-leave calculations
  are reviewed there.

## Permissions and access

- **Employees** permission is required for the private HR menus.
- Separation and final-settlement records are sensitive. Limit access to HR and finance staff who need
  them.
- Document access follows classification and ownership; see
  [Documents and workflow](../shared/documents-and-workflow.md).
- Review the audit trail when access or approvers change.

## Common questions

| Question | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Why can't I publish a vacancy? | The requisition must be approved. See [Private recruitment](private-recruitment.md). |
| Why is an offer not available? | The application should be at `recommended`. |
| Why can't I hire the applicant? | The offer must be accepted and the applicant must not already be hired. |
| Why can't I regularize the employee? | Check the probation review and employment type in [Private employment](private-employment.md). |
| Why can't the separation become effective? | Clearance and final settlement must be reviewed and approved. See [Private separation](private-separation.md). |
| Why is the retirement amount missing? | Use the Retirement calculations; see [Retirement](../shared/retirement.md). |
